**F.R.I.E.N.D.S** is an American television sitcom created by David Crane and Marta Kauffman, which aired on NBC from September 22, 1994, to May 6, 2004, lasting 10 seasons. The series revolves around six friends in their 20s and 30s living in Manhattan, New York City, as they navigate their personal and professional lives.

# Main Characters
**Rachel Green** (Jennifer Aniston): A fashion enthusiast who transforms from a spoiled runaway bride into a successful career woman.

**Monica Geller** (Courteney Cox): A obsessive-compulsive, highly competitive chef who acts as the mother hen of the group.

**Phoebe Buffay** (Lisa Kudrow): An eccentric, free-spirited masseuse and self-taught musician known for her iconic song "Smelly Cat".

**Joey Tribbiani** (Matt LeBlanc): A lovable, food-obsessed struggling actor best known for his catchphrase, "How you doin'?"

**Chandler Bing** (Matthew Perry): An executive in statistical analysis and data reconfiguration who uses sarcasm as a defense mechanism.

**Ross Geller** (David Schwimmer): A sweet, neurotic paleontologist with a passion for science and a complicated, long-standing romantic history with Rachel.
